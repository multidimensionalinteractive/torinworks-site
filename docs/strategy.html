At-a-Glance Summary

Reposition the practice around higher-value smile transformation work

RANUNCULUS DENTAL has recently transitioned ownership and is in the early stages of rebranding toward a new audience. The practice currently has low visibility in search, a broad patient mix, and limited systems for lead capture and fast follow-up. The opportunity is to reposition the practice around smile transformations, supported by sedation and comprehensive care, while building the visibility, conversion systems, and case portfolio needed to attract larger, higher-value cases.

Retargeting: high priority
Implement retargeting across Meta and Google so prospective patients continue seeing RANUNCULUS DENTAL after visiting the website or engaging with ads. For a practice with low initial brand recognition, repeated exposure is critical. Seeing the practice multiple times builds familiarity, trust, and perceived authority—especially for cosmetic procedures with longer decision cycles.
Why this matters
Patients are unlikely to convert on their first visit. Retargeting ensures the practice remains visible during the consideration period and bridges the gap between initial interest and booked consultation while accelerating brand recognition in the local market.
SEO & Content Strategy

Build the site around high-intent search behavior

Lead with outcomes

Use language such as Smile Makeover, Whiter Teeth, Straighter Teeth, and Natural-Looking Results rather than relying only on clinical procedure names.

Target high-intent keywords

Prioritize Smile Makeover, Veneers, Whitening, Invisalign, plus modifiers such as cost, near me, and before & after.

Own local search

Include location-based language consistently across the site, such as Cosmetic Dentist in Carlsbad.

Use before & after content heavily

Before-and-after imagery is one of the strongest drivers of both SEO performance and conversion. Optimize image names and captions with relevant keywords and integrate results throughout service pages.

Capture patient problems and emotion

Build content around concerns such as chipped teeth, gaps, uneven smiles, or staining, and pair this with emotional outcomes such as confidence, feeling good about your smile, and natural-looking results.

Highlight speed and convenience where applicable

Use language such as same-day smile makeover or fast teeth whitening when clinically appropriate and supported by the service offering.

Content expansion
Build a blog or resource section over time to capture additional traffic and support authority. Focus on cost questions, comparisons, treatment education, and patient decision-stage questions.
Simple SEO formula
Transformation + Outcome + Location + Emotion
Example: “Transform Your Smile with Veneers in Carlsbad — Natural, Confident Results”
